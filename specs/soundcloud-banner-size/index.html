SoundCloud banner size

Three placements, three different specs, and three different official sentences. Here is what SoundCloud actually publishes, quoted.

Profile headerat least 2480 × 520 px, 2MB limit
Track artworkat least 800 × 800 px, under 2MB
Playlist artworkup to 2MB, any size

The header: "for best results", not a floor

SoundCloud's wording matters more than most size guides admit. The header page does not call 2480 × 520 a requirement.

"For best results, upload JPG or PNG images of at least 2480 x 520 pixels with a 2MB size limit."
— Update Your Profile Image and Header

So: 2480 × 520 is the size you want. Write it as for best results, or as at least 2480 × 520. Do not present it as a floor — the page does not say that, and the difference shows up when a smaller upload is still accepted.

Track artwork: this one is a spec

"Specs: Upload a .jpg or .png (at least 800 x 800 pixels) under 2MB."
— Edit and customize your tracks

Note what is absent: no "for best results". This sentence opens with "Specs:" and states the floor. Write at least 800 × 800 pixels. Softening it into a suggestion tells readers that 600 px will do, and it will not.

Playlist artwork: a third sentence, a third limit

"We accept any images up to 2mb in size."
— Creating and customizing a playlist

Three placements, three sources, and the 2MB limit is worded three different ways: with a 2MB size limit, under 2MB, up to 2mb in size. Quote each one against its own page. Collapsing them into a single "2MB file limit" inventing a spec SoundCloud never wrote.

Safe area: SoundCloud publishes none

The header help page warns that text gets cropped on smaller screens. It does not give an edge, a rectangle, or a percentage that survives every device.

So there is no official safe area to cite. You can still work one out for your own layout — the diagram above uses a 120 px side band and a 70 px top and bottom band as a drawing convention — but do not present it as SoundCloud's number, because it is not.

Practical rule: keep your name, logo and any lettering away from the edges. Then upload and look at the result on a phone. That check takes ten seconds and beats any margin you computed for a device you do not own.

Uploading from a phone

SoundCloud's header guide includes iOS and Android steps: open your profile, edit it, tap the current header. The file preparation happens wherever you like; the save happens in SoundCloud.

Why the header will not zoom

Zoom is unavailable when the selected image is smaller than 1240 × 260 pixels. If you are fighting the zoom control, your upload is under that floor. Best results still means at least 2480 × 520.

Compression after upload

The cited pages give no final encoded quality and no promise that the file survives unmodified. Check the copy you downloaded, then check the live profile separately. Those are two different artifacts.

Playlist artwork does not cascade

Changing a playlist image does not update the covers of the tracks inside it. The playlist guide says so. If you replaced the playlist art and the tracks still look old, that is expected.

Questions

Which side gets cropped on a phone?

SoundCloud names no edge and no safe area. Test your own saved profile on your own device.

Is 2480 × 520 mandatory?

No. The guide says "for best results". It does recommend that size, and it is what I would upload.

Can I upload from the app?

Yes. Open your profile, edit, tap the header.

Does new playlist artwork replace every track cover?

No.
